## Key Insights

### Sales Trends
Q4 recorded the highest quarterly sales at approximately $587K, while Q1 recorded the lowest at approximately $261K.

### Regional Performance
The West region was the strongest performer with approximately $522K in sales, followed by East with approximately $450K.

The South region recorded the lowest sales at approximately $252K.

### Category Performance
Office Supplies was the leading category with approximately $644K in sales, followed by Technology ($471K) and Furniture ($452K).

### Product Performance
The dashboard identifies the top-performing and lowest-performing products, helping highlight products that contribute most to sales and those that may require further investigation.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate the factors driving strong Q4 sales.
- Develop targeted strategies to improve South region performance.
- Leverage the strong performance of Office Supplies through cross-selling and upselling.
- Review pricing, demand, and inventory strategies for low-performing products.
- Identify successful strategies in high-performing regions and apply them where appropriate.
